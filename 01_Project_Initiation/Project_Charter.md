## 2. Executive Summary

Emma-Origbo Hub is a planned dynamic web-based information platform designed to help students, graduates, career changers and early-career professionals discover, organize and understand education, skills-development and career opportunities.

The platform is intended to bring relevant opportunity information into a more structured and accessible environment. Potential content areas include educational programmes, internships, scholarships, bootcamps, certifications, career resources, digital tools and other professional development opportunities.

The project will be developed as a realistic portfolio-scale Information Systems project. It will combine business analysis, requirements engineering, process analysis, information architecture, database design, software development, testing, analytics and documentation.

The project will begin with business and user understanding rather than immediate software development. The system requirements, processes, data structures, user interactions and technical architecture will be defined before implementation.

---

## 3. Project Background

Students, graduates and early-career professionals frequently need to search across multiple sources to identify relevant educational programmes, internships, scholarships, certifications, skills-development programmes and career resources.

Information may be distributed across websites, social media platforms, institutional pages, newsletters, messaging channels and other digital sources.

This fragmented environment can make opportunity discovery time-consuming and can make it difficult for users to compare opportunities, determine eligibility, identify deadlines and decide which opportunities are relevant to their goals.

Emma-Origbo Hub is proposed as a structured digital solution to this information-discovery problem.

The project will explore how information about education, skills and career opportunities can be organized, categorized, searched, filtered and presented through a purpose-built information system.

## 4. Business and Problem Context

### Current Situation

Opportunity information is often distributed across different sources and presented in different formats.

Users may need to:

- Search multiple websites.
- Monitor social media pages.
- Check institutional announcements.
- Read lengthy opportunity descriptions.
- Determine whether they meet eligibility requirements.
- Track application deadlines manually.
- Compare opportunities from different providers.
- Save useful opportunities for later reference.

### Business Opportunity

A structured platform could improve the way opportunity information is organized and accessed.

Instead of treating opportunities as disconnected pieces of information, the proposed platform can provide a centralized information structure in which opportunities are categorized using attributes such as:

- Opportunity type.
- Provider.
- Target audience.
- Field or career area.
- Location.
- Delivery mode.
- Eligibility.
- Application deadline.
- Cost.
- Required qualifications.
- Opportunity status.

This structure can support more effective discovery, filtering and decision-making.

## 5. Problem Statement

Students, graduates, career changers and early-career professionals may struggle to efficiently discover and evaluate relevant education, skills-development and career opportunities because information is fragmented across multiple sources and often lacks a consistent structure.

The absence of a centralized and structured discovery system can result in:

- Difficulty finding relevant opportunities.
- Excessive time spent searching.
- Difficulty comparing opportunities.
- Missed application deadlines.
- Difficulty determining eligibility.
- Difficulty distinguishing opportunity types.
- Information overload.
- Poor organization of saved opportunities.
- Limited visibility into opportunity trends.

Emma-Origbo Hub will investigate and address these challenges through a structured digital information platform.

## 30. Ethical, Legal and Content Considerations

The platform must avoid presenting unverified information as guaranteed or authoritative.

Where opportunity information is sourced from external organizations:

- The original provider should remain identifiable.
- Official application sources should be used where appropriate.
- Information should not be intentionally misrepresented.
- Expired information should be identified or removed.
- Copyrighted content should not be reproduced improperly.
- Personal information should be handled responsibly.
- User data should only be collected where justified.
- The platform should not imply endorsement by an opportunity provider unless such endorsement actually exists.

The platform is intended to assist discovery and information organization; users remain responsible for verifying opportunity information with the official provider before applying.

## 32. Project Limitations

The project has several anticipated limitations.

First, the project is being developed at portfolio/simulation scale and therefore may not represent the infrastructure, traffic volume or operational complexity of a production commercial platform.

Second, real-time opportunity information may not always be available.

Third, where actual operational data is unavailable, representative or simulated data may be used.

Fourth, the project cannot guarantee the accuracy, availability or continued existence of information published by third-party organizations.

These limitations will be clearly documented in the final project materials.

## 34. Project Quality Principles

The project will follow these principles:

1. **Business before technology** — understand the problem before building the solution.
2. **Requirements before implementation** — define what the system must do before coding.
3. **Data before database implementation** — identify business entities and relationships before writing database tables.
4. **Evidence before assumptions** — distinguish researched facts from project assumptions.
5. **Validation before progression** — verify completed work before moving to the next phase.
6. **Documentation throughout** — document important decisions and results during development.
7. **Small, testable changes** — implement and verify components progressively.
8. **Professional Git practices** — maintain meaningful version history.
9. **Honest portfolio representation** — never present simulated outcomes as real business results.
10. **Controlled scope** — evaluate proposed changes before adding them to the project.
