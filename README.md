---

## 1. Project Overview

The ECHO Education & Career Discovery Platform is a planned dynamic web platform designed to help students, graduates, career changers and early-career professionals discover and understand education, skills-development and career opportunities.

The platform will combine structured opportunity information with regularly updated editorial content, including educational programmes, internships, scholarships, bootcamps, certifications, career resources, digital tools and related opportunities.

The platform is intended to operate as both an information and content platform and a transparent affiliate/referral marketing business.

---

## 2. Business Problem

Information about educational programmes, internships, scholarships, bootcamps, certifications and career opportunities is often fragmented across different websites and platforms.

Users may also face difficulties determining:

- whether an opportunity is relevant to them;
- whether information is current;
- whether an opportunity is legitimate;
- what eligibility requirements apply;
- when an opportunity closes;
- what an organisation or programme actually offers; and
- where to find the official application or programme information.

The platform aims to address these challenges through structured information, editorial guidance, source verification and transparent commercial disclosures.

---

## 3. Target Users

- University and tertiary students

## 5. Trust and Editorial Principles

The platform is intended to maintain user trust by:

- prioritising authoritative and official sources;
- verifying important information where practical;
- recording verification dates;
- identifying expired or closed opportunities;
- distinguishing verified information from unverified information;
- avoiding misleading claims;
- avoiding unsupported claims that one organisation or product is universally "the best";
- clearly disclosing affiliate and referral relationships; and
- allowing users to make their own informed decisions.

Commercial relationships will not be presented as independent editorial endorsements.

## 11. Project Objectives

The project aims to develop a credible, maintainable and scalable information platform that:

1. Helps users discover relevant education, skills and career opportunities.
2. Provides structured and understandable programme information.
3. Improves transparency around opportunity verification.
4. Provides regularly updated editorial content.
5. Supports transparent affiliate/referral relationships.
6. Demonstrates professional business-analysis, database, software-development and information-systems practices.
7. Provides a realistic portfolio project that can evolve beyond the initial MVP.

---

## 12. Development Principle

The project will prioritise:

**Business need → Requirements → Design → Implementation → Testing → Verification**

Technology decisions will be justified by project requirements rather than selected solely because they are popular or technically advanced.
